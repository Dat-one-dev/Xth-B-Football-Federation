How It Stays Fair

1. Fisher-Yates Shuffle

Every roll starts with a pure random shuffle of the selected players, so no two rolls are ever the same.

2. Skill Power Rating

Each player is rated by points per match from real season stats, so a proven scorer weighs more than a one-match wonder.

3. Noise Handicap

Each rating gets a ±12% random jitter before drafting — still random, but strong players can't fully dodge their strength.

4. Snake Draft Balance

Strongest pick goes to Team 1, next two to Team 2, next two to Team 1… alternating like a real draft, keeping team power nearly equal.

5. Keeper Split

The top goalkeepers (best saves per match) are spread across both teams so one side never ends up keeper-less.

6. Captain Auto-Select

The highest rated player on each team automatically becomes the captain.

7. Fairness Swap Pass

Small pairwise trades are tried until the two teams' total power sits within 8% of each other — the safety net that catches any bad roll.
